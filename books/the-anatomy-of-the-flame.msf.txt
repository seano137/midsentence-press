§1 EGYPT_AND_SCRIPTURE
c1.1 [p=0.99] Ancient Egyptians identified the heart (Ib) as the primary seat of thought, moral intelligence and spiritual accounting, weighed against the feather of Ma'at.
c1.2 [p=0.98] The Book of Enoch details a celestial and subterranean cosmology in which human consciousness interacts directly with angelic and demonic spiritual entities.

§2 EAST_AND_WEST
c2.1 [p=0.99] Eastern traditions (Vedanta, Buddhism, Yoga, Taoism) map consciousness across subtle energy channels (Nadis), energetic centres (Chakras) and states ranging from waking to deep sleep. ~c1.1
